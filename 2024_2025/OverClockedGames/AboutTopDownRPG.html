What is the 'Top Down RPG'?

'Top Down RPG' is the placeholder name for the first game set in th universe of the 'Overclocked Games' universe. It takes place after the backstory of Project S.

It's a game game with a lot of different dimensions and two different camera-anges: wide third person and top down. One angle is for fighting, one for exploring.

The artstyle is more drawn/comiclike. The cutscenes look like someone is drawing them as they play out, sometimes the camera even zooms out of the game onto a piece of paper, on which the story is drawn.

Where does the game take place?

The game startes in a different dimention then the one in Project S in a rural area close to a big forrest. As the story progresses, the player visits multipe dimentions.
