ProjectS Biomes

The game is taking place on an island far from civilisation.

Since the game will be pretty long we have settled on the idea of different biomes to make place for a lot of variation.

At the start we came up with a lot of normal biomes that are often used in Fantasy games, such as forests, planes, big cave systems and mountains.

We wanted the world to feel very large, so we decided to use many large landscapes.

Since the game should be taking place in a fantasy world with magic and monsters, there also had to be other, more vibrant and exotic biomes...
