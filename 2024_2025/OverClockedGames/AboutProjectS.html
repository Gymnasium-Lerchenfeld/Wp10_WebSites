What is "Project S"?

"Project S" is the codename for the main game of Overclocked Games.
The "S" stands for "Soulslike game".

It's a third-person open world game with a big combat and story aspect. More on that later.

Where does the game take place?

The game takes place on an island far away from any land or civilization. It's devided into multiple biomes, each one a different biome theme, e.g. a desert.
